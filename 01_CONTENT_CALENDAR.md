## SEO Architecture

Har maqola quyidagilarni o'z ichiga oladi: **1 Primary Keyword · 5–10 Secondary Keywords · Semantic Entities · FAQ · Meta Title · Meta Description · Optimized Slug · Featured Image · ALT Text · Internal Links · CTA · Schema.**

### Topical Authority Model

`1 Pillar → 5–10 Supporting → Examples → Templates → FAQs → Advanced Topics → Industry Applications` — har klaster to'liq topical ekotizim hosil qiladi.

### Knowledge Graph

`Financial Analysis → Financial Statements → Balance Sheet → Profit & Loss → Cash Flow → Working Capital → Budgeting → Forecasting → KPIs → Dashboards → Virtual CFO → Corporate Finance → Business Growth`

### Internal Link Strategy

Har maqola bog'lanadi: Previous Articles · Related Articles · Pillar Articles · Category Landing Pages · Recommended Reading. **Orphan sahifalar taqiqlanadi.**

### CTA Rotation Matrix

Request Financial Diagnosis · Book a Virtual CFO Consultation · Download Financial Template · Read Related Guide · Contact DigitalCFO · Request Dashboard Demo · Business Financial Review. *Ketma-ket bir xil CTA takrorlanmaydi.*

### Content Quality Rules

Har maqola o'tishi shart: SEO · Grammar · Financial Accuracy · EEAT · Readability · Internal Linking · Metadata · Human Writing audit. **Minimal ball: 95/100.**

### Publishing Rules

Hech qachon takrorlanmaydi: Topics · Titles · Introductions · FAQs · CTAs · Examples · Tables · Conclusions.
